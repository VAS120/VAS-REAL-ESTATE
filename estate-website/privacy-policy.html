Global Privacy & Data Governance Policy

Our binding legal commitment to confidentiality, digital security, and fiduciary privacy.

Home Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 4.2

1. Fiduciary Commitment to Discretion

At VAS Estates Luxury Real Estate ("VAS Estates Luxury Real Estate," "we," "us," or "our"), safeguarding the personal, financial, and architectural confidentiality of our clientele is paramount. We hold ourselves to institutional standards that transcend basic legal requirements, recognizing that high-value real estate transactions demand absolute discretion.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to conduct legitimate brokerage advisory and fulfill anti-money laundering (AML) compliance:

  • Principal Identification: Full names, residential and corporate addresses, passport/government ID copies (exclusively for escrow anti-fraud verification).
  • Financial Comfort Assurances: Banker comfort letters, proof-of-funds statements, and entity formation documentation required by sellers before off-market disclosure.
  • Property & Acquisition Parameters: Architectural preferences, target budgets, and geographic mandates.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, encrypted cookies, and device identifiers strictly used to authenticate client portal sessions.

3. Absolute Non-Sale of Data

VAS Estates Luxury Real Estate has never sold, licensed, or shared client or visitor data with commercial third-party aggregators, advertising exchanges, or lead brokers, and will never do so under any circumstance. Your information remains strictly within our internal encrypted transaction rooms.

4. Non-Disclosure & Bilateral Protocols

All off-market listings, floor plans, and seller particulars shared with registered clients are governed by binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Similarly, all buyer criteria, offers, and transaction amounts are kept strictly confidential from public media unless explicit joint consent is granted by both buyer and seller.

5. Cross-Border Governance (GDPR, CCPA & Swiss FADP)

In accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), our clients maintain the right to:

  • Request full inspection of all data records maintained by our fiduciary desk.
  • Demand immediate expungement of non-statutory records following closing completion.
  • Opt out of marketing intelligence distributions at any time.

6. Security & Encryption Standards

All communications transmitted through our client portal are fortified with 256-bit AES TLS encryption at rest and in transit. Document repositories are isolated in sovereign SOC2-compliant data centers with multi-factor biometric authentication.
